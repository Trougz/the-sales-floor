\#\# Transcript
 
If your mind ever goes completely blank during a cold call, I want you to use these three simple phrases to keep the prospect talking while your brain catches up. Number one, tell me more about that. Cold calling is simple. I’m going to give you guys my top three cold call openers that I use every single day. And number three is my personal favourite.
 
Number one:
