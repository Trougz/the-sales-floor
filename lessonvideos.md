\# Core Lessons
 
\#\# 1\. “Following Up” Weakens Your Message
 
The phrase is overused in sales and signals low-value, repetitive outreach. It reduces perceived intent and authority.

Reframing outreach increases engagement by making the message more relevant, conversational, and outcome-oriented.

\> “Hey John, I’m just curious— is this still a priority for you guys right now or should we revisit this at a later date?”

\> “Hey John, I just wanted to close out the loop here. Should we move forward with this or should I close out your file for now?”

\- “Following up” is a low-impact phrase that weakens communication.  
\- Strong messaging creates clearer buyer responses.  
\- Follow-ups should push for clarity, not passive engagement.  
\- Reframing outreach improves response rates and authority.  
\- Closing loops or reconnecting creates stronger momentum than generic check-ins.
